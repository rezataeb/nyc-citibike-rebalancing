The problem

An empty or full station is a barrier to choosing a bike.

12.2%
of 451,111 station observations were empty or full (Jul 13 – Sep 13, 2026)

The City's Citi Bike contract, as summarized by the NYC Comptroller, requires stations not to sit completely full or empty, with a penalty only when adjacent stations are also out. This tool turns that rule into something a planner can see.
