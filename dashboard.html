        Glossary
        Double-outage
        A station with 0 bikes or 0 docks that also has another empty/full station within 500 m — a compounding failure, not just one broken station.
        Net daily flow
        A station's average net arrivals minus departures at a given hour, in bikes/day — averaged across the days observed, not a 24-hour total.
        Elasticity
        How much a station's bike flow shifts with temperature and precipitation, fit from a real regression against a full year of historical weather and ridership.
        Equity gap
        How much worse live service is at stations near NYCHA housing, schools, or far from a subway, compared with other stations right now.
        Typology
        A station's behavioral archetype — e.g. “Commuter core” or “Residential feeder” — from clustering real hourly flow patterns, not from neighborhood or borough.
        Service availability (now)
        The share of stations not currently in double-outage — an instant reading, not the 60-minute measurement the underlying contract actually defines.
        Public data: Citi Bike GBFS & trip data, NYC Open Data, Open-Meteo. Independent portfolio project, not an official NYC DOT product.
